Video Compressor

Squeeze a video under a size limit. It all happens in this browser tab, so your video never gets uploaded.

Much, much slower than the desktop app. The browser has to read the video one frame at a time, so a 1 minute video can take several minutes. For long or large videos, use the Windows desktop app instead.

Pros

  • Nothing to install. Open the page and go.
  • Works on iPhone, Android, Mac, Linux and Chromebook, not just Windows.
  • Your video never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Free, with no sign-up.
  • Same target sizes and the same automatic retry when the result comes out too big.

Cons

  • Much, much slower. A 1 minute video can take several minutes.
  • One pass instead of two, so the same size can look a bit worse.
  • Caps videos at 30 fps.
  • The tab has to stay open and in front the whole time.
  • Needs a recent browser. Older ones can't encode video.
  • Less format support. MKV, AVI, FLV and WMV often won't open.
  • Very large videos can run out of memory, especially on phones.

Short clip, any device: use this page. Long or large video, or you need the best quality at a tight size: use the Windows desktop app.

This browser can't encode video (it needs WebCodecs). Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Web version of the Video Compressor desktop app. It uses the same sizing rules, but it encodes in a single pass with your browser's built-in H.264 encoder, and it is far slower than the desktop app's two-pass FFmpeg. If the result lands over the target, it retries at a lower bitrate.
